How the studio actually works

Not a booking desk. A small production office that holds the full line.

Bhanshree Creatives is the firm you call when an idea has to become a night, a film, a campaign or a feed — and someone has to own vendors, crew, timeline and the last detail. We write the run-of-show, book the PA, lock the shot list, and stay until strike.

Typical briefs: a 70–400 guest gathering, a product launch with live social, a 60-second brand film, a 90-day Instagram retainer, or a short-film script before cameras are hired.
