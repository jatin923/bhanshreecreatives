About us

A studio for ideas that need to be seen, felt and remembered.

Bhanshree Creatives is a creative media, event management and production firm founded by Harshit Sharma. We work across creative and professional domains, helping brands, businesses, artists and communities bring their ideas to life.

Our vision is a creative agency that holds strategic thinking, storytelling, digital media and real-world experience under one brand — capable of taking a brief from first concept through complete execution.
